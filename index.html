About Medios'26

Where Media Meets Ideas

Medios'26 is a premier national media festival and summit organized by Jamia Madeenathunnoor under the overarching theme "Casting Mass Commune".

Set in the scenic green highlands of Imam Suyuthi College of Integrated Studies (Paleri Usthad Academy), Tharuvana, Wayanad, the conclave brings together students, media enthusiasts, creators, communicators, and thought leaders for high-voltage learning, competitive exhibitions, and creative dialogue.

  • National-level competitive tracks for budding journalists and visual storytellers
  • Hands-on masterclasses and workshops led by acclaimed media professionals
  • Rigorous debates dissecting journalism ethics, digital narratives, and AI in media

Agenda

Conclave Schedule

A two-day dynamic timeline spanning masterclasses, screenings, contests, and valedictory awards.

08:30 AM – 09:45 AM Registration

Delegate Reception & Kit Handover

Check-in at the Main Atrium, ID badge verification, and Medios'26 welcome package distribution.

10:00 AM – 11:30 AM Grand Inaugural

Inaugural Session: "Casting Mass Commune"

Presided by Leadership of Jamia Madeenathunnoor & Guest Editors

Formal launch of Medios'26, keynote vision addresses, and souvenir release ceremony.

11:45 AM – 01:15 PM Symposium

Panel Debate: The Algorithmic Feed vs. Ethical Truth

Lead Columnists and Digital Media Theorists

Examining the impact of content feeds, algorithmic curation, and generative text on editorial integrity.

02:00 PM – 05:00 PM Contests

Live Competition Phase: On-Spot Writing & Photo Walk

Participants set out on campus assignments for News Reporting, Photography, and Graphic Layout tracks.

05:30 PM – 07:00 PM Masterclass

The Craft of Investigative Journalism

Veteran Field Reporter

Deep-dive into source protection, fact-verification, open-source intelligence, and long-form writing.

09:00 AM – 11:00 AM Screenings

National Short Film & Podcast Finals

Screening of shortlisted student short films and audio documentary finalists with live jury feedback.

11:15 AM – 01:00 PM Quiz

The Grand National Media Quiz Finals

Conducted by Guest Quizmaster

High-stakes buzzer rounds on media history, cinema, press law, advertising, and current affairs.

02:00 PM – 03:30 PM Forum

Future of Media: Visual Culture & Impact

Interactive session on utilizing visual storytelling and documentary mediums for socio-cultural impact.

04:00 PM – 06:00 PM Valedictory

Valedictory Ceremony & Excellence Awards

Chief Guests & Academy Leadership

Conferment of cash awards, trophies, certificates, and adoption of the Medios'26 Declaration.
